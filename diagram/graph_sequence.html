System Output Specification

The final output is NOT a greenwashing score. The system returns an advisory Evidence Dossier containing:

  • Sustainability Claim: Extracted report claims
  • Supporting Evidence: Verified third-party corroborations
  • Contradicting Evidence: Controversies, penalties, & media reports
  • Advisory Assessment: appears_supported | appears_contradicted | unverified_insufficient_evidence
  • Provenance: Page numbers, sentence indexes, source domains & URLs
  • Temporal Information: Canonical ISO dates & validity windows
  • Caveats: Methodological & data coverage limitations

Note: The final decision always belongs to a human analyst.
